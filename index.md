I am Jinhyeok Jeong (I usually go by Jin), a Ph.D. candidate in Psychological Sciences (Cognition and Cognitive Neuroscience) at Vanderbilt University, advised by [Dr. Thomas J. Palmeri](http://catlab.psy.vanderbilt.edu/).

### Research Interests

I am broadly interested in visual cognition, particularly how visual information is perceived and represented, and how humans use these representations to make decisions. I use behavioral experiments and computational cognitive modeling to investigate the computational mechanisms underlying visual cognition and behavior.

My current research focuses on two related areas:

- **Ensemble perception.** I am developing and testing a computational modeling framework that jointly accounts for choice and response time distributions across different ensemble perception tasks.

- **Perceptual categorization.** I am investigating how representations derived from deep neural networks can be integrated with cognitive decision models to explain human object categorization behavior.

I am also interested in extending these computational approaches to other domains of visual cognition, exploring new questions about the representations and processes underlying human vision and decision-making.

### Background

I received my B.A. in Psychology and M.S. in Cognitive Science from Yonsei University. During my master's studies, I worked with [Dr. Sang Chul Chong](https://vcc.yonsei.ac.kr) to investigate ensemble perception, particularly the perception of visual variability, using psychophysical methods.
